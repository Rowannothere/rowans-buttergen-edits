<strong>Buttergen v0.3</strong>

New:
- Buttergen now runs on the newest development versions of Genemod, LifeGen and ClanGen
- Physical traits passed down as genes, from Inheritable Physical Traits by Snow
- About 3,000 more names, from Various Names
- 48 rare special eye colors passed down as genes, from Beetle's Eyes
- White patch shapes can show up as tortie patches and the reverse, as inherited genes, from Mink's Torties
- Dark white patches as a rare inherited gene, from Simple Dark Patches. Mustaches are extra likely to get them
- More experience titles on profiles, from Trainee to Grandmaster, from More Info
- Over 300 new personality traits, from Foxies Traits Galore
- 7 new border and training patrols, from Theo's Patrols
- More biting relationship moments between cats, from Meaner ClanGen
- Editing a relationship now has its own button on each cat's card

Fixes:
- All harnesses show the right color in every pose, and the pink, peach and violet ones show up at all
- Moon skips no longer crash on an event that names the leader of a Clan with no leader
- The speaker button goes back to its spot after leaving the cat maker
- The music and ambiance keep playing when the cat maker opens from inside a Clan
